![War-Horse](/assets/knowledge/items/bow-trails/war-horse.png)

Dyed horse armour, patterned shields, and now... bow trails!

Just put the bow in the crafting table along with whatever colours you want the trail to be, and voilà!

![Recipe](/assets/knowledge/items/bow-trails/recipe.png)

Want a new set of colours? Just put the bow back in the crafting table with the new dyes and the trail will reset to the new palette.
