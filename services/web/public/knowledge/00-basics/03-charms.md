Charms are powerful magical artifacts of divine origins. Their powers range from the **simple but effective** (such as granting extra hearts), the **helpful** (such as insta-mining ore veins and trees), to the **strange** (such as climbing on walls and ceilings, or jumping in mid-air).

You can find charms by looking for rare magic books, sometimes spawning in treasure chests. Look out for the dark teal cover:

![A buried treasure chest with a magic book spawned](/assets/knowledge/items/util_staves/buried_treasure.png)

Read the magic book to learn about a powerful charm. You will then receive the charm as well as being able to buy more copies in the shop from that point onwards.

## Using charms

Charms are meant to be equipped in specific armor slots. There are chestplate, boots and leggings charms.

![Equipped charm example image](/assets/knowledge/basics/charms/equipped_charm.png)

To be both protected by armor while enjoying the effects of charms, you can combine charms with armor:

![Combining charmor example image](/assets/knowledge/basics/charms/combining_charms.png)

Now, equipping this copper chestplate will grant the same effects as equipping the charm alone previously did.

To seperate the armor and the charm again, just put the armor piece in the crafting table:

![Seperating charmor example image](/assets/knowledge/basics/charms/seperating_charms.png)

When you take the charm out of the crafting result slot, the chestplate will stay behind in the input grid. The charm and the armor piece will be seperated again.

:::info
You cannot put the same charm type on the same piece of armor twice.
:::

### Equipping multiple charms in one armor piece

Having one charm per slot is cool, but it does limit you to having only 3 charms active at once, and is pretty annoying.

The **Charm Boost Enchantment** can fix this for you. When applied to a piece of armor, it will allow that armor piece to hold more than 1 charm, by adding more charm slots:

![Equipped charm example image](/assets/knowledge/basics/charms/applying_charm_boost.png)

Different armor types are better than others at conducting magic. Here's how many charm slots each armor set has with and without the Charm Boost Enchantment:

| Armour | Slots without Charm Boost | Slots with Charm Boost |
| :-- | :--: | :--: |
| Leather, Copper, Chainmail, Iron | 1 | 2 |
| Diamond | 1 | 3 |
| Gold, Netherite | 1 | 4 |
| [Enderite](/play/knowledge/enderite) | 1 | 6 |

To get your hands on the Charm Boost enchantment, you need to find a Charm Boost Enchanted Book, which spawn buried in Sand or Gravel in Archeological digsites. (e.g. Trail Ruins, Desert Pyramids, Desert Wells, Ocean Ruins).

## Upgrade a charm

Almost all charms start out with decent power, but can be leveled to be very powerful. To do so:

1. Check the charm tooltip for the dabloons and items required to upgrade the charm. Get all that stuff together in your inventory.
2. Hold the charm in your main hand. If it is stored in armour, seperate the armour first.
3. Open the [Charm Forge](/play/charms) on the website.
4. Select **Refresh inventory** after you started holding the charm.
5. Keep the required ingredients in your inventory and select **Upgrade charm**.

And then, enjoy your new abilities! (And the mega-fancy animations)
