# Food

:::drop-indicator

All of the foods in vanilla feel so plain, I mean, why just leave it at cooked cod and baked potatoes when you could easily turn that into fish and chips! We fixed this by added all new foods - which you can also place down as decoration in your builds.

### Corn, Grapes and Tomatoes

Farmer villagers are now able to trade corn, grapes and tomatoes, useful ingredients in making certain food dishes. Eaten raw they aren't the best though.

# Magic Powder

Magic powder can be bought in the shop and crafter together with certain foods to recieve special effects, from a greater chance to find certain types of fish, to getting discounts in the shop.
