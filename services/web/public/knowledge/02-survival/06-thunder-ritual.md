# Thunder Ritual

:::drop-indicator

Whilst rain doth fall and thunder yet lies still,\
Set five by five beneath the open sky.\
One heart of gold; eight amethysts enclose it;\
At their four corners, full-grown clusters rise.

Twelve cut-copper stairs gird the outer sides,\
Three to a side, each facing toward the heart.\
Four copper blocks hold fast the outer corners;\
Each bears one upright lightning rod.\
Let wax and weather touch none of this copper.

Upon the gold, a lectern bears one book,\
Enchanted with Channeling, and naught besides.\
Stand upon the lectern; gaze near straight to heaven,\
And cast thy trident upward through the rain.

The voice shall name its tribute; cast it within the square.\
When paid, the rain shall learn to thunder.
