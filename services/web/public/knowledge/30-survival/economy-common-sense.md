## Economy Common Sense

If the server develops shops, trades, or society events, remember that custom items may change the value of normal resources.

An item that saves time can be economically powerful even if it does not directly create blocks or ores. Convenience has value.

Be careful with trades involving rare charms. If you do not understand what a charm does yet, do not trade it away casually.
