![War-Horse](/assets/knowledge/items/bow-trails/war-horse.png)

It's super easy to add awesome particle trails to many different tools: your bow, crossbow, elytra, tridents, or mace!

![Elytra Example](/assets/knowledge/items/bow-trails/elytra-example.png)
 
## Add, mix, or clear particles

Each type of particle is tied to a specific item. When you combine the item with the tool in a crafting table, the trail will be applied.

![Recipe](/assets/knowledge/items/bow-trails/recipe.png)

To get rid of all the particles saved on a tool, craft the tool alone without other ingredients:

![Recipe tool clearing](/assets/knowledge/items/bow-trails/tool-clearing.png)

You can add as many different items to the tool as you want.

If you add two 2 red dye items and 1 blue dye item to the tool, the tool trail will consist of twice the amount of red dust particles than blue dust particles.

## Ingredients

### Colored Dust

Colored dust is the basic particle, created by different dyes.

Combining a tool with red, green and white dye would for example create:

![Target-Practice](/assets/knowledge/items/bow-trails/target-practice.png)

:::perk
Normal players can use colored dust particles, but members can additionally use all the other cool particles there are in the game!
:::
