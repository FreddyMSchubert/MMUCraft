Charms are powerful magical artifacts. Use a magic book to discover a charm. The book changes into the new charm at its minimum level. You can buy more copies of a discovered charm from the shop.

To upgrade a charm, hold it in your main hand and open the Charms page on the website. Refresh the page after you change the held item. The page shows the required ingredients and [dabloon](/play/knowledge/money-basics) price. Select **Upgrade charm** to upgrade it. The server checks the held charm, removes the ingredients and dabloons, and gives the charm its next level.

To equip or remove a charm, use the combine or separate recipe for the correct armour slot. The crafting table does not upgrade charms.

## Charm Boost

All armour starts with one charm slot, including [Enderite armour](/play/knowledge/enderite). Charm Boost unlocks the armour material's additional charm slots. Find the enchanted book by brushing suspicious sand or suspicious gravel at any archaeology site; each block has a 25% chance to contain one.
