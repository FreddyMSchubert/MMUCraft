Golden Nutritional Paste is the best food in the game. Drink one to restore 10 hunger bars, fill your saturation, and heal 16 hearts. You can drink it even when your hunger bar is full.

## Crafting Golden Nutritional Paste

Use one of each ingredient below in a crafting table. The recipe is shapeless, so their positions do not matter.

![Golden Nutritional Paste Crafting Recipe](/assets/knowledge/items/golden_nutritional_paste/recipe.png)

:::recipe-items
[Cookie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cookie) · [Beetroot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Beetroot) · [Pumpkin](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pumpkin) · [Gold Nugget](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Gold_Nugget) · [Bowl](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bowl) · any fish · any mushroom · any fungus · any raw farm-animal meat → Golden Nutritional Paste
:::

The recipe returns the bowl after you drink the paste. It is expensive, but it takes ingredients you will often have lying around by the late game. At least in theory, it should be fully auto-farmable, to let you become completely indestructible.
