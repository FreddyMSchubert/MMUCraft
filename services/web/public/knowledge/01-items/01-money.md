Money makes the world go round, including the Minecraft world, which is neither round nor rotating. (I assume...)

The currency used on this server is called dabloons. (<span class="dabloonAmount dabloonTone-inherit" aria-label="Dabloon symbol">&#xF0DAB;</span>) They come in the forms of actual Minecraft Coin Items, in your inventory. (So, you know, dying with a bunch of money is probably a bad idea.)

![Some of the lower-value coin textures](/assets/knowledge/basics/money/coins.png)

:::tip
To avoid coins clogging up your inventory, use a [wallet](/play/knowledge/wallets).
:::

:::tip
To avoid losing your wallet when you die, enchant it with [Soulbound](/play/knowledge/soulbound).
:::

## Using the dabloons

You can combine multiple lower-value dabloon coins into one higher-value dabloon coin:

![Compress dabloons by putting enough coins of the same type into the crafting table](/assets/knowledge/basics/money/compress.png)

:::recipe-items
1 Dabloon ×5 → 5 Dabloons
:::

You can also uncombine them in reverse:

![Decompress dabloons by putting the coin to decompress in the crafting table](/assets/knowledge/basics/money/decompress.png)

:::recipe-items
5 Dabloons → 1 Dabloon ×5
:::

## Earning dabloons

You can earn dabloons through a variety of methods:

### 1. **Completing advancements**

Advancements give you Dabloons for completing them.

You can see how many dabloons a given advancement will earn you before completing it by looking at it in the advancements menu. The server has over 1000 additional advancements beyond the Vanilla Minecraft ones. ([All advancements overview](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1_DwKEZ0vqCOp2POhiOVSoMVeVNpU1WNPzk0L8qR_y2s))

:::tip
The vanilla advancements menu is really mid and very cramped, so it's recommended you use some [client side mod to improve the advancements menu](https://modrinth.com/discover/mods?q=advancement&v=26.2&e=client) if you know how to mod your game.
:::

:::tip
Completing an advancement on Sunday will give you an increased amount of Dabloons.
:::

:::perk
Members get a permanent boost to how much money they get from completing advancements, even if it is not Sunday.
:::

### 2. **Daily Streak**

Every day you can get Dabloons just for logging into the server. You can claim this reward on the [Dailies Tab](/play/dailies).

Every consecutive day you log into the server, this reward increases. There is ***NO CAP*** to the amount of Dabloons you can earn from this.

When you miss a day, your streak gets reset to 0.

:::perk
When members miss a day, instead of their streak resetting to 0, their streak only gets cut in half.
:::

### 3. **Dailies**

Every day you get a few random tasks given to you.

They pay quite a lot of dabloons, especially if you manage to complete all of the ones assigned to you on a given day:

![Full Dailies Completion Example](/assets/knowledge/basics/money/dailies_full_completion.png)

:::tip
Completing all dailies on a Sunday will give you an increased amount of Dabloons as a completion reward.
:::

:::perk
As a member, you get even more Dabloons when completing all Dailies on a given day.
:::

### 4. **Trading**

Stealing from players is not allowed. Trading is. Good luck, try not to get scammed.

### 5. **Reading knowledge**

Reading knowledge is supremely useful.

Beyond that, it is also a great monetary choice, because each read knowledge book gives you 3 dabloons.

### 6. **Gift Codes**

Are occasionally handed out at events such as freshers fair. They are time limited codes, which pay you out some money if you type it into the website. Check the "*Misc*" tab for all infos.

### 7. **The secret method** - (we colloquially call it corruption)

Buy the committee a drink (for legal reasons, this is a joke, and will not work.)

:::info
If you find a bug and report it, we may also reward you some dabloons - though this is handled on a case-by-base basis and you are not owed anything by finding a bug. If you find a bug and don't report it, you'll get banned. Also, that's mean.
:::

## Spending dabloons

### 1. **Buying stuff**

Buying items from the [shop](/play/shop) costs dabloons. You can buy powerful magical artifacts ([Charms](/play/knowledge/charms)), a variety of awesome [cosmetics](/play/knowledge/cosmetics), and much more.

### 2. **Buying claims**

Claims are critical to protect your items & builds.

In your claims, nobody except for the players you choose can
- break or place blocks
- open chests, barrels etc.
- interact with anything at all

This includes other mobs like creepers.

For all infos, check out the [claims tab](/play/claims) on this website.

#### Claim Cost

Every consecutive claim you buy costs more and more money.

To buy the next claim, you have to pay 1.69 times as much as you paid for the last.

:::perk
For members, claims are cheaper - every next claim only costs 1.42 times as much as the last one cost.
:::

### 3. **Trading with others**

Try not to get scammed.

Good luck!
