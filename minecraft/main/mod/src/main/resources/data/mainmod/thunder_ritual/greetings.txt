Mortal, you have raised copper to the heavens. Speak, and I shall judge what the clouds owe you.
I heard your trident pierce the sky. Now hear the price of my thunder.
The rods are awake, and the amethyst sings. Come closer, supplicant.
You have called upon the heights. Let us see whether your offering is worthy.
The sky has many ears, but tonight it is I who answers.
