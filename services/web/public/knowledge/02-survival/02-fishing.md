## How to catch a fish

1. Hold a fishing rod and right-click water to cast.
2. When the bobber is pulled down in a large splash, right-click once.
3. The catch flies back to you. Cast again.

![Fishing bite](/assets/knowledge/survival/fishing/catching_example.png)

Fishing can be sorta slow at the start. To make things quicker:
- Use the [Lure](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lure) Enchantment on your rod.
- Fish during rain & without blocks above your head

Some fish are rarer than others. There are 6 rarity levels: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythical.

Not every fish can be caught in every scenario. Some fish may only be catchable in ocean or river water, and some may only be catchable during rainy nights.

The full overview of every fish catchable and caught is the [fishing compendium](/play/fishing). It also explains the conditions required to catch each individual fish.

## How to catch what your looking for

Each cast rolls either a **fish** or an **item**. There are modifiers that can change the fish/item chances in the following ways, when held in your offhand while casting:

| Offhand item | Result |
| :-- | :-- |
| Nothing | 65% fish, 35% item |
| Worm | 80% fish |
| Golden Worm | 100% fish |
| Item Magnet | 55% item |
| Golden Item Magnet | 75% item |

![Fishing Worms Example](/assets/knowledge/survival/fishing/worms_example.png)

Worms and Magnets can be found hidden in suspicious sand and suspicious gravel respectively. They somewhere after you fished in that area for a while. Use a brush to get them out.

To get the golden versions, either get lucky when brushing, or upgrade them:

## How to catch better fish

The higher your luck stat, the better your chances of catching higher-rarity fish and items. The total luck currently achievable on the server is 11. You can get more luck as follows:

1. [Luck of the Sea](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Luck_of_the_Sea) III on your rod gives 3 Luck.

The easiest way to get your hands on Luck of the Sea is finding a LotS Enchanted Book, one of which spawns in every buried treasure chest. [More Info here](/play/knowledge/enchanting-and-anvils).

- A [Lucky Charm](/play/knowledge/charms) on your chestplate gives up to 5 Luck at level 5.
- A Potion of Luck gives 3 Luck. It is a large boost, so save it for hunting a hard fish or a record session.

You can get a potion of luck like this:

![Luck Potion Brewing Recipe](/assets/knowledge/survival/fishing/luck_brewing.png)

:::recipe-items
First apply [Nether Wart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Wart), then a 4-leaf-clover → Potion of Luck
:::

To get clovers, you'll need a [Sniffer](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer). They can find 1,2,3 and 4 leaf clovers while digging. Luckily, you can craft the clover leaves together in arbitrary ways to make 4-leaf-clovers:
