# Charm hiding and armour appearance

:::drop-indicator

Not the biggest fan of how your charm looks on your armour set? Don't like your hand-crafted skin being covered by ugly armour? 
Have I got news for you!

![Alex](/assets/knowledge/aesthetics/armour-hiding/alex.png)
<sub>Alex wearing a full set of minimised diamond armour, with the rib armour trim on all pieces</sub>

## Charm Hiding

If you combine a piece of armour with a glass pane in a crafting table, you can disable the visibility of your equipped charms.

![Hiding Charm](/assets/knowledge/aesthetics/armour-hiding/charm-recipe.png)

To undo this change and make your charms visible again, just combine it with a glass pane again.

:::note
This works for all pieces of armour except for helmets, as they do not allow for charms.
:::
## Armour minimising

The same can be done with glass blocks to minimise your armour, leaving behind just a small, stylish trim letting others know that they should NOT mess with you.

![Minimising Armour](/assets/knowledge/aesthetics/armour-hiding/minimise-recipe.png)

To have your armour visible again, combine it with a glass block again.

## Armour hiding

Put a piece of armour in the centre of a crafting table and surround it with eight tinted glass blocks to hide the armour entirely. Craft it the same way again to show the armour. Charms remain visible unless you hide them with a glass pane; minimising takes effect again when you show the armour.
