## Fashion Books

(image of book in loot chest)

You may have noticed the "Fashion Books" in loot chests, when right clicking these books you will unlock a cosmetic at random that you can then purchase from the shop. 

These hats don't do anything but make you look extra fashionable, so don't worry about trying to min-max for the best one!

## Equipping

If you're worried about losing armour while wearing your hat, don't be:

![Cosmetic Combining](/assets/knowledge/items/cosmetics/cosmetic-combining.png)

You can combine your cosmetic with any helmet! It will retain the protection and enchants that your helmet has, while levelling up your looks. 
Better yet, if you break your helmet, your cosmetic stays on your head.

![Cosmetic Separating](/assets/knowledge/items/cosmetics/cosmetic-separating.png)

Once you've had enough of one cosmetic - or want to change for a better helmet - just put the cosmetic back into the crafting table. This will give you the cosmetic, and the helmet will be left in the grid like buckets when making cake.

## Dyeing

Some cosmetics allow you to [dye](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dye) them, whichs means you can colour it however you wish.
This works just like normal minecraft dyeing: put it in a crafting table with a dye and out comes a coloured version! (You can even apply multiple dyes at once to get a more specific shade)

![Dyeing](/assets/knowledge/items/cosmetics/dyeing.png)

Do note that all cosmetics come pre-coloured. 
Want to add your own coat of paint (or fix a botched dye-job)?
You can wash it in a cauldron by just right clicking!

![Cosmetic over cauldron](/assets/knowledge/items/cosmetics/cauldron.png)
![Cleaned cosmetic](/assets/knowledge/items/cosmetics/clean.png)

## As Decoration

Some cosmetics can be placed in item frames to look like props, combine this with [invisible item frames](/play/knowledge/invisicarrot) and you can make a nice, cozy base.
