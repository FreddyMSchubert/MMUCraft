Charms are powerful magical artifacts. Use a magic book to discover a charm. The book changes into the new charm at its minimum level. You can buy more copies of a discovered charm from the shop.

To upgrade a charm, hold it in your main hand and open the Charms page on the website. Refresh the page after you change the held item. The page shows the required ingredients and dabloon price. Select **Upgrade charm** to upgrade it. The server checks the held charm, removes the ingredients and dabloons, and gives the charm its next level.

To equip or remove a charm, use the combine or separate recipe for the correct armour slot. The crafting table does not upgrade charms.
