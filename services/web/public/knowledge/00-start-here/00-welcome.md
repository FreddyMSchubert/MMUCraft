## Welcome to the MMU Minecraft Society Server Knowledgebase

This page grows as you play. Some information is visible immediately, but other notes, tricks, recipes, history entries, and mechanical details are unlocked by finding and using knowledge books in-game.

Locked sections are intentionally scrambled. When you unlock one, the real text appears here permanently for your account.

- Be respectful to other players.
- Report exploits rather than abusing them.
- Ask staff if a custom mechanic seems broken.
- Try unusual items. Many fake items have behaviour that is not obvious from vanilla Minecraft.
