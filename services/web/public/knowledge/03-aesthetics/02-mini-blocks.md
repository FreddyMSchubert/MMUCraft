**Mini blocks are mini versions of blocks. 👍**

They can be great for decorating:

![Mini Blocks Example](/assets/knowledge/items/mini-blocks/example.png)

### There's two ways to get them:

1. Craft it from the larger block in a stone cutter:

![Stonecutter Recipe](/assets/knowledge/items/mini-blocks/crafting.png)

2. Trade for them with a wandering trader:

![Wandering Trader Trading Example](/assets/knowledge/items/mini-blocks/wandering_trades.png)

### How do I know what mini blocks there are?

You don't. But you can see glimpses of what blocks there are when you meet a wandering trader and you can experiment with putting different blocks in a stonecutter.

There's a lot of cool mini blocks to find, not only tiny existing blocks, but also other decorations.

For an example of whats possible with Minecraft Player Heads, check out [minecraft-heads.com](https://minecraft-heads.com/player-heads).

![Mini Blocks Sniffer Egg Example](/assets/knowledge/items/mini-blocks/wandering_trades.png)
