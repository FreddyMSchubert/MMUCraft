These recipes make building and transport items easier to get.

## Smelting

- Smelt one [Rotten Flesh](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Rotten_Flesh) into one [Leather](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leather).

## Rails

- Six [Iron Ingots](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Ingot) and one [Stick](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stick) make 64 [Rails](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Rail).
- Six [Gold Ingots](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Gold_Ingot), one Stick, and one [Redstone Dust](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Redstone_Dust) make 16 [Powered Rails](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Powered_Rail).
- Six [Copper Ingots](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Copper_Ingot), one Stick, and one Redstone Dust make 16 Powered Rails.
- Six Iron Ingots, one [Stone Pressure Plate](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pressure_Plate), and one Redstone Dust make 16 [Detector Rails](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Detector_Rail).
- Six Iron Ingots, two Sticks, and one [Redstone Torch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Redstone_Torch) make 16 [Activator Rails](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Activator_Rail).

## Minecarts

- Five Iron Ingots make four [Minecarts](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecart).

Minecarts with no attachment, chests, furnaces, TNT, and hoppers stack to 64.
