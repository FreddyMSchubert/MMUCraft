Of course, since Minecraft is a childrens game, youd never get killed. Right? 👀

Anyways, for those of you that are experiencing occasional skill issues, here's what we changed about dying on the server:

## Respawning

After death, you no longer respawn next to your bed, but closer to wherever you died. This is mainly because walking a bunch is annoying, not fun.

Specifically, you respawn at a random position **within the server render distance** of where you died. This means that when you respawn, you are always able to see the position you died at (unless it's in a cave, behind a hill, or your render distance is set lower than the server render distance).

![Death Screen](/assets/knowledge/survival/death-respawning/death_screen.png)

:::note
When dying to the void in the end specifically, you keep all your items and respawn at world spawn.
:::

## [Soulbound](/play/knowledge/soulbound)

The [Soulbound enchantment](/play/knowledge/soulbound) makes it possible to keep certain items on your person after death. The following items can be enchanted with it:
- Tools, Weapons & Armor
- [Wallets](/play/knowledge/wallets) (Which is very useful so you don't lose your hard-earned dabloons)
- [**Recovery Compasses**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Recovery_Compass) (Which is very useful because respawning somewhere unknown can be disorienting, and they always point to your last death location)

(For more info and the crafting recipe, read the [Soulbound Enchantment](/play/knowledge/soulbound) entry.)
