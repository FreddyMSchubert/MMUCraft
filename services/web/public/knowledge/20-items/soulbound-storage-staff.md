## Soulbound Storage Staff

Some staff items can open personal storage. The Soulbound Storage Staff opens your ender chest inventory from wherever you are.

This does not create a shared society storage. It is still your own ender chest. Anything you place inside follows normal ender chest behaviour.

Because the item opens a real inventory menu, be careful when using it near danger. Mobs, lava, or other players will not politely wait for you to finish organising your items.
