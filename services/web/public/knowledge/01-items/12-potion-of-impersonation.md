# Potions of Impersonation

![Potion of Impersonation Example Image](/assets/knowledge/items/potion-of-impersonation/result.png)

You can make yourself temporarily look like another player by drinking an Impersonation Potion.

All you need as a potion ingredient is their head. Each player drops their head when they [die](/play/knowledge/death-and-respawning).

Then use it as a potion ingredient (note the bottles at the bottom need to be awakward potions).

![Potion of Impersonation Recipe](/assets/knowledge/items/potion-of-impersonation/recipe.png)

If you now drink it, you can turn into the other player!

- Redstone increases the potions duration from 5 to 15 minutes
- Gunpowder turns it into a splash potion so you can transform other people
- Dragons breath makes it a lingering potion
