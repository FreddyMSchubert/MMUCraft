*I mean, if Mojang won't do it...*

Enderite tools and armor are powerful because they are able to hold significantly more magic than Netherite equipment. That means:
1. They have higher max level caps for enchantments. For example, a Netherite helmet can only have Respiration up to level 3. An Enderite helmet on the other hand can have Respiration up to level 10, making for effectively infinite underwater breathing. Check out the [Enchanting Knowledge Book](play/knowledge/enchanting-and-anvils) for the full overview.
2. For Enderite Armor specifically, it has two more [Charm slots](/play/knowledge/charms) per armor piece when Charm Boost is applied, making for a total of 6 charm slots. Finally, all the charms at once!

## How to get it

Enderite can be found in the End, with it occurring more commonly near the bottom of end islands. In fact, it is quite common to see a few Alien Debris Ore blocks visible when dropping down below End islands.

In this image, you can see it on the right side (dark green):

![Enderite mining example image](/assets/knowledge/survival/enderite/mining_example.png)

:::tip
A spyglass or a zoom mod can be very helpful when alien-debris-hunting.
:::

Once Alien Debris is mined, it works pretty similarly to Ancient Debris. That means, the first step is to smelt it into Enderite Scraps:

![Alien Debris to Enderite Scraps Smelting example image](/assets/knowledge/survival/enderite/smelting_recipe.png)

:::recipe-items
Alien Debris → Enderite Scraps
:::

These Enderite Scraps can then be turned into Enderite Ingots, using this recipe:

![Enderite Ingot Recipe](/assets/knowledge/survival/enderite/ingot_recipe.png)

:::recipe-items
Enderite Scraps x4 · [Block of Gold](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Gold) x4 → Enderite Ingot
:::

Once you've obtained some Enderite Scraps, you'll also need an Enderite Upgrade Smithing Template. They can be found in End City chests. Once you found a single one, you can duplicate the Template like this:

![Enderite Upgrade Smithing Template Duplication Recipe](/assets/knowledge/survival/enderite/template_dupe.png)

:::recipe-items
[Diamond](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Diamond) (TL, TR, CL, CR) · Enderite Upgrade Smithing Template (TC) · [End Stone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_Stone) (CC) · [Block of Diamond](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Diamond) (BL, BR) · [Netherite Ingot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherite_Ingot) (BC) → Enderite Upgrade Smithing Template x2
:::

Once you have arranged all of that, you can now upgrade your Netherite Armor and Tools into Enderite, for example:

![Smithing Template Enderite Apply Recipe](/assets/knowledge/survival/enderite/smithing_apply.png)

:::recipe-items
Enderite Upgrade Smithing Template · Any piece of Netherite Equipment · Enderite Ingot → Enderite Equipment
:::

Congrats! Can you feel the power?
