# Magic Powder

Magic powder can be bought in the shop and crafter together with certain foods to recieve special effects, from a greater chance to find certain types of fish, to getting discounts in the shop.

## Fishing Effects

| Food | Dust Required | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fish and Chips | 6 | Higher chance to catch uncaught fish |
| Sushi | 3 | Higher chance to catch common fish |
| Crab Claw | 4 | Higher chance to catch uncommon fish |
| Tentacle | 5 | Higher chance to catch rare fish |
| Fish Stew | 6 | Higher chance to catch epic fish |
| Esquixada | 7 | Higher chance to catch legendary fish |
| Big Seafood Salad | 8 | Higher chance to catch mythical fish |

## Books

| Food | Dust Required | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buzzbloque | 1 | Higher chance to find joke books |
| Tea | 2 | Higher chance to find knowledge books |
| Cocktail | 4 | Higher chance to find fashion books |
| Wine | 7 | Higher chance to find charm books |

## Money

| Food | Dust Required | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Milkshake | 2 | All shop items are 10% off |
| Burger | 3 | All shop items are 20% off |
| Saturn Burger | 4 | All shop items are 30% off |
| Roasted Marshmallows | 5 | Advancements and dailies give the same amount of money as they would on a Sunday |

:::note Keep in mind
These effects do not stack, if you get a 10% discount and then eat a Burger, the 20% discount will override the previous 10%. It does NOT add together 30%.
:::

# Food

:::drop-indicator

All of the foods in vanilla feel so plain, I mean, why just leave it at cooked cod and baked potatoes when you could easily turn that into fish and chips! We fixed this by added all new foods - which you can also place down as decoration in your builds.

### Corn, Grapes and Tomatoes

Farmer villagers are now able to trade corn, grapes and tomatoes, useful ingredients in making certain food dishes. Eaten raw they aren't the best though.
